## Ezra's Stremio 3.5.0 Beta 1

- Open **YouTube** from the sidebar to browse and play videos in YouTube's own player.
- Unzip UltraWideoVideo, then choose **Install blur extension** and select the folder containing `manifest.json`. Keep that folder where you selected it so the browser can load it again.
- Open **Blur settings** during playback to adjust the extension. Press **Alt+Shift+V** to open the settings in fullscreen.
- The YouTube tab uses its own browser profile. This beta is available on the **Beta** update channel.
