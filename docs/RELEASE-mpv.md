## Ezra's Stremio 2.1.1

- The volume popup stays hidden from the moment the player opens, including the brief period before mpv finishes loading. Manual volume changes still show it.
- The information drawer opened with **I** uses 6% background transparency in both themes. Its text and controls stay fully opaque.
- **Library → Manage lists** lets you rename, reorder, and delete custom lists and choose which lists appear on Home. Backups can export and import list membership and manual Anime or Live Action assignments.
- **Picture** groups shader, border blur, and framing controls. It also offers per-series preferences, source switching with optional resume, and copyable playback diagnostics.
- The fork version appears alongside the other version numbers in Settings. Windows also preserves Unicode filenames when opening local videos.
