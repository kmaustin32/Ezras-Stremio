## Ezra's Stremio 2.0.0 Beta 1

Organize your Library with named lists and see them on Home after Featured Movies.

- Choose **Create list** at the top right of Library. Pick a list in the same dropdown as **All**, **Movie**, and **Series**.
- Choose **Add to list** in a title's three-dot menu, then select one or more lists. **Ctrl+click** posters to add several titles at once. In a list, choose **Remove from this list** to adjust membership.
- **Anime** and **Live Action** start empty and have shortcuts between **Z–A** and **Most Watched**.
- **Watched** and **Unwatched** now show only matching titles, including saved titles beyond the first Library page.

Lists and their memberships are saved on this device for the signed-in account. The existing Library and add-on export does not include custom lists.
