## Ezra's Stremio 2.1.3

- **Skip Intro** and **Skip Outro** use embedded chapters even when the video reports them after playback starts.
- Optional TheIntroDB lookup accepts small runtime differences within a matching release group. Skip buttons stay available when the player updates its URL options and clear when switching to another video.
